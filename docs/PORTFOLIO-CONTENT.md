# 7. Content presentation guidelines

- Lead with selected work rather than a long biography.
- Use short, specific project summaries on the homepage.
- Give complex technical projects dedicated case-study space.
- Explain the problem, approach, contribution, and outcome.
- Distinguish team achievements from personal contributions.
- Show authentic visual evidence.
- Include technical depth without requiring every visitor to have domain expertise.
- Avoid excessive technology badges and long résumé-style lists.
- Never invent quantitative improvements or results.
- Do not publish unfinished claims or unconfirmed details as facts.
