Overview

This university project brought computer vision and autonomous behavior together on a Unitree Go2. My role combined technical development with Scrum coordination in a team of four.

My contribution

  • Evaluated six vision models, including Qwen-VL and Microsoft Phi.
  • Integrated YOLO object detection.
  • Contributed to perception, autonomous behavior, integration, testing and troubleshooting.
  • Facilitated sprint planning, standups and retrospectives.

Approach

The documented work spans model evaluation, object detection and integration with the robot. Evaluating models was one part of the project; integrating and testing the software was another.

The team research poster below documents the system architecture and model selection. This summary does not attribute the entire system to my individual work.

Status and limitations

The demonstration documents development testing, not general-purpose autonomy. The team poster records hardware resource constraints, tracking reliability challenges and inference latency; its measurements should be read alongside those limitations.
